# Opportunity-Test and Prospect-Tracking Structure

**Status:** ACTIVE OPERATING STRUCTURE
**Owner:** ChatGPT, commercial / operating lead writer
**Founder authority:** Andre

## Purpose

Create one consistent structure for testing the CRE Acquisition Systems and non-CRE Revenue Systems wedges, directing immediate outreach, and preventing internal activity from being mistaken for market proof.

Human commercial evidence governs advancement. Every stage change needs a dated evidence reference and a named human owner for the next action.

## System-of-record boundary

This file defines the structure; it is not the live CRM.

The approved operating system of record should hold prospect identity, contact details, interaction history, and authorized client information. This repository may hold test definitions, generalized learning, anonymized prospect IDs, and aggregate evidence.

Never store credentials, raw client data, PII, proprietary deal information, or client acquisition intelligence here.

## Evidence-controlled lifecycle

| Stage | Meaning | Minimum evidence required | Does not qualify |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Selected | Prospect meets the current test criteria. | Supportable selection rationale and wedge assignment. | A scraped name with no fit rationale. |
| 1 — Outreach active | A relevant message was sent through an approved channel. | Dated send record and next-action date. | Drafted copy or intended outreach. |
| 2 — Human response | A human replied or directly engaged. | Dated interaction record stating what the person actually said. | Opens, impressions, automated replies, or agent interpretation. |
| 3 — Discovery scheduled | The person agreed to a problem conversation. | Human-confirmed meeting or equivalent conversation commitment. | A suggested time with no acceptance. |
| 4 — Problem confirmed | The human described a material workflow bottleneck and its impact. | Discovery evidence naming the workflow, problem, owner, and stated consequence. | Generic interest in AI or a demo. |
| 5 — Qualified opportunity | The problem, authority, urgency, economic plausibility, and safe access path support further work. | Completed qualification fields and a clear next action accepted by the human. | Pain without authority, urgency, economics, or access. |
| 6 — Scoped requirement | The discovery finding has become a bounded business requirement. | Written scope candidate, success measure, dependencies, exclusions, and client responsibilities. | A discovery note or technical idea. |
| 7 — Feasibility confirmed | The technical owner has evaluated the scoped requirement. | Technical feasibility result, risks, dependencies, and acceptance approach. | Commercial assumption about what can be built. |
| 8 — Proposal authorized | A priced scope is approved for client presentation. | Founder approval plus the approved proposal artifact. | A price mentioned conversationally or a draft prepared by a seat. |
| 9 — Signed / won | The client authorized the engagement. | Executed agreement or other governed signed authorization. | Verbal enthusiasm or an unsigned proposal. |
| Closed — No current fit | The opportunity should not advance now. | Human-stated reason, exhausted bounded outreach sequence, or Founder decision, with date. | An unexamined assumption about silence or fit. |

## Operating cadence

1. Select prospects against an active test.
2. Begin outreach immediately; technical demonstration work proceeds in parallel.
3. Record every consequential human interaction in the approved system of record.
4. Advance only when the minimum evidence for the next stage exists.
5. Route scoped requirements for technical feasibility before making a client commitment.
6. Review CRE and non-CRE evidence against the provisional 70/30 allocation.
7. Route consequential scope, price, allocation, hiring, and acceptance decisions to Andre.
