# Founding Systems Partner Offer

**Status:** ACTIVE COMMERCIAL TEST
**Owner:** ChatGPT, commercial / operating lead writer
**Founder authority:** Andre
**Applies to:** CRE Acquisition Systems and qualified non-CRE Revenue Systems opportunities

## Purpose

The Founding Systems Partner offer is Vandtage's initial paid engagement for an operator with a measurable revenue or acquisition bottleneck that can be addressed through a bounded AI-enabled operating system.

The offer is designed to answer two questions with real client evidence:

1. Can Vandtage materially improve one important workflow?
2. Can the client support an ongoing **operate + measure + improve** relationship at **$3,500+ per month**?

This is an active market test. It is not a permanent price card, a universal scope, or a promise that every prospect is technically feasible.

## Positioning

Vandtage helps operator-led businesses stop losing valuable opportunities inside fragmented intake, qualification, follow-up, routing, analysis, and visibility workflows.

The first CRE expression is:

> **CRE Deal Intake → Qualification → Follow-Up → Deal Read Routing**

The non-CRE expression applies the same operating logic to qualified revenue systems where weak intake, response, qualification, follow-up, routing, or visibility causes valuable opportunities to leak.

GoHighLevel or another configurable platform may be part of the infrastructure. The product is the governed operating system and measured business outcome, not the software license.

## Best-fit partner

A strong Founding Systems Partner has:

- a repeatable, commercially important workflow with a named owner;
- an observable bottleneck rather than a general desire to "use AI";
- enough opportunity volume or value for improvement to matter;
- access to the people, process, and approved systems needed to diagnose the workflow;
- willingness to establish a baseline and review evidence during the engagement; and
- authority and budget to approve a scoped engagement if the case is real.

Poor-fit conditions include no identifiable operating problem, no accountable workflow owner, no safe access path, a request for unbounded transformation, or an expectation that Vandtage will promise results before discovery and feasibility review.

## 90-day engagement shape

The engagement follows the governed sequence **SELL → SPECIFY → BUILD → VERIFY → OPERATE**.

### 1. Diagnose and baseline

- identify one measurable acquisition or revenue bottleneck;
- map the current workflow, handoffs, failure points, and owner;
- define the starting baseline using evidence the client can support; and
- agree on the business outcome and bounded success measures.

### 2. Specify and approve

- translate the discovery finding into a scoped business requirement;
- complete a technical feasibility check;
- state dependencies, exclusions, client responsibilities, and acceptance criteria; and
- obtain Founder-approved priced scope and signed client authorization before implementation.

### 3. Build and verify

- configure or build the approved workflow;
- instrument the measures required by the agreed outcome;
- test against the governed acceptance criteria; and
- use independent technical verification when the claim or risk requires it.

### 4. Operate, measure, and improve

- observe the live workflow during the initial term;
- review the agreed measures with the client;
- correct bounded defects and improve the approved system; and
- use the evidence to decide whether to continue, expand, narrow, or stop.

## Active pricing test

Preferred structure:

- **$3,500–$5,000 setup**
- **$3,500/month managed engagement**
- **90-day initial term**

Alternative structure:

- **$5,000/month for 3 months**

These prices are tests, not permanent canon. Any exception, custom commitment, or final commercial structure requires Andre's approval. The client proposal must make billing timing, inclusions, exclusions, and any third-party costs explicit.

## What is included only after scope approval

The signed scope may include a bounded combination of workflow design, configuration, custom implementation, integration, measurement, operating review, and improvement. Inclusion is determined by discovery, feasibility, price, risk, and Founder approval.

The offer does not automatically include:

- an unlimited number of workflows, locations, teams, or integrations;
- third-party software or usage fees;
- access to client credentials or data outside an approved access method;
- unverified performance guarantees;
- work using client-confidential information for NOVUS acquisition activity; or
- technical commitments made before feasibility review.

## Advancement rule

Human commercial evidence governs advancement.

An opportunity advances only when a dated human interaction or executed client artifact supports the next stage. Internal enthusiasm, copy completion, a demo, an agent's inference, or technical possibility is not market evidence.

The minimum progression is:

**Human response → discovery finding → commercial opportunity → scoped requirement → feasibility check → priced scope → signed authorization → implementation**

The applicable evidence reference and next action must be recorded in the opportunity tracker. Consequential pricing, scope, and acceptance decisions remain with Andre.

## Confidentiality and record boundary

This repository may hold generalized commercial learning and anonymized evidence summaries. It must not hold credentials, raw client data, PII, proprietary deal information, or client acquisition intelligence. Live prospect and client records belong in the approved operating system of record.
