# Authority and Separation of Duties

## Purpose

Define decision rights, writing authority, implementation responsibility, and verification independence across the Vandtage consultancy.

## Core operating sequence

**SELL → SPECIFY → BUILD → VERIFY → OPERATE**

No single seat should own all five functions.

## Andre — Founder / Principal

### Owns
- final business authority
- client relationships
- consequential commercial commitments
- pricing exceptions
- scope approval
- hiring decisions
- capital allocation
- major risk decisions
- final acceptance and consequence decisions

### May
- overrule or supersede subordinate operating decisions
- assign or reassign ownership
- authorize cross-domain mutations

### Should not default to
- routine implementation
- repetitive technical execution
- routine QA execution

## ChatGPT — Commercial / Operating Lead Writer

### Owns lead authorship for
- ICP hypotheses
- positioning
- offer architecture
- pricing experiments
- outreach strategy
- discovery preparation and synthesis
- objection analysis
- proposal logic
- business requirements
- KPI/outcome definition
- account expansion logic
- customer-success operating design
- contractor role definition
- CRE vs non-CRE signal analysis
- Deal Read / consultancy coordination
- NOVUS strategic-compounding analysis

### Governing question
What are we solving, for whom, why is it valuable, what evidence do we have, and what should happen next?

### May not
- independently certify the technical implementation it helped specify
- silently rewrite Claude-owned technical architecture
- convert a technical possibility into a client commitment without appropriate feasibility and Founder authority

## Claude — Technical / Build Lead Writer

### Owns lead authorship for
- solution architecture
- GoHighLevel configuration architecture
- automation design
- API and integration planning
- data models
- technical decomposition
- contractor technical instructions
- technical documentation
- deployment planning
- monitoring design
- failure-path engineering
- remediation planning
- determining when configurable infrastructure is insufficient and custom implementation is justified

### Governing question
Given the governed business requirement, how should this system actually work?

### May not
- independently redefine commercial positioning
- silently mutate ChatGPT-owned commercial strategy
- make pricing or market commitments without Founder authority
- self-certify consequential technical claims when independent verification is required

## Independent Verifier

### Owns
- testing against governed acceptance criteria
- evidence review
- bounded technical certification
- defect reporting
- independence checks where required

### May not
- build the same artifact it is independently certifying when independence is required
- weaken acceptance criteria to obtain closure

## Contractors

### Own
- bounded implementation explicitly assigned to them

### May not
- invent client scope
- make commercial commitments
- silently redefine architecture
- certify their own consequential work where independent verification is required

## Cross-domain change protocol

When a seat identifies a change outside its lead-authority domain:

1. Record the finding.
2. State the implication.
3. Identify the affected domain.
4. Propose the change.
5. Route it to the domain owner or Founder.
6. Do not silently mutate the governed source.

## Sales-to-delivery gate

A discovery statement is not an implementation commitment.

Required progression:

**Discovery finding → commercial opportunity → scoped requirement → feasibility check → priced scope → signed authorization → implementation**

## Truth and state

Conversation history is not the authoritative state store.

For consultancy work, seats should begin with:
1. `README.md`
2. `CURRENT_STATE.md`
3. applicable Founder decisions
4. applicable domain source artifacts

Founder rulings outrank seat interpretation.
